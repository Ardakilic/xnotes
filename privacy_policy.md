# xNotes Privacy Policy

xNotes stores your notes, settings, and handle records only in your
browser's local storage.

If you enable sync, an encrypted copy of your notes is sent solely to
the WebDAV or S3 server address you enter yourself. Your passphrase
never leaves your device and is never stored anywhere.

xNotes collects, transmits, sells, or shares no data with the developer
or any third party. It uses no analytics, loads no remote code, and
contains no advertising.
